Lock reliability and icon fixes.

• The lock now engages reliably when you send Balance to the background on devices that kept the session open.
• Open menus (Data, Language, sort and bank options) no longer stay on top of the lock after the app is reopened.
• Opening About and coming back no longer asks for your PIN or fingerprint again.
• The fingerprint icon on the lock screen now renders correctly.
• The lock code is masked in both entry fields while you type it.
• The auto-mask eye and the lock icon now use a green "active" color, so you can tell at a glance when auto-masking and the app lock are on.
