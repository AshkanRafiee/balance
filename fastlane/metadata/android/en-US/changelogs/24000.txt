Balances per account number, and Melli movements fixed.

• When a bank message states the account number, balances are shown per account — each bank card is split into one row per account, and the bank total is derived from its accounts.
• Tapping an account row or the account chip in the history screen opens a single-account history; long-pressing an account card copies that account's exact balance.
• History filters and the combined total respect the same per-account split, and long-deposit/withdraw totals per day, month and year stay consistent with it.
• Melli movements are now read from their own label and sign, so the first movement of an account is no longer skipped and later balances are no longer off by it.
