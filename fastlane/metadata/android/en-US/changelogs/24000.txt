Per-account balances, history filters with a Persian calendar, and a scan-diagnostics funnel.

• When a bank message states the account number, balances are shown per account — each bank card is split into one row per account, and the bank total is derived from its accounts. Tapping an account row opens that single account's history, and long-pressing an account card copies its exact balance.
• Melli movements are now read from their own label and sign, so the first movement of an account is no longer skipped and later balances are no longer off by it. Account detection runs through a single shared engine that recognizes account numbers in Tejarat, Parsian, Melli, Saderat, Pasargad and Mehr Iran messages, and each account's chain of movements stays intact across refreshes and rule updates.
• History filters by movement type (all, deposits or withdrawals) and by date — today, this month, this year or a custom range picked on a month-grid calendar — applied to every figure on the screen.
• Scan diagnostics (the Report item in the footer): see which bank SMS parse per bank and which do not — known banks with an unreadable message layout are flagged first, then senders not yet recognized. Tap any flagged sender to open that sender's messages in a chooser and tick exactly which to send or copy (nothing is preselected); you can also mark what is wrong — account, balance or sender-number detection — and it goes into the prefilled email so the maintainer knows which stage failed. The email is only sent after you approve in the system chooser.
• Excluding a bank or account now excludes exactly that entry, not every account of the bank.
• The balance list gains pull-to-refresh, kinetic scrolling, and follows your system font size; balances refresh automatically as bank SMS arrive, with a watermark that also covers a clock set ahead.
• The new "Display" menu in the footer holds its selectors: the Calendar setting switches the app's calendar system between the Persian (Jalali) calendar for Iran (the default) and the Gregorian calendar, with localized month and weekday names for both; the Currency setting picks the value and unit shown next to amounts — Toman by default (dividing the rial figure by ten as before), or Rial, or a custom currency, which show the raw number under their own label; the Language selector overrides the interface language; and the Theme setting switches between the system default, dark and light.
• Copied balances are cleared from the system clipboard after a minute, and account numbers are masked wherever balances are hidden.
• Backups now require an 8-character minimum password; lockout cooldowns track the monotonic clock, and the empty screen offers a retry and a direct path to SMS settings.
• About gained a donate row pointing to the README donation page, plus accessibility polish (darker accent contrast, screen-reader announcements for the lock keypad and widget, letter-spaced headings only in left-to-right layouts).
