Teach Balance to read your bank. If it does not know the messages from your bank yet, you can now write the rule yourself: paste one of its messages, select the amount, the balance and the date, and Balance writes the rule, tries it against that same message and shows you exactly what it read before anything is installed.

• Write a rule for your bank: open "Write a rule", paste a message from your bank and select the parts of it — the amount, the remaining balance, the date, an account number — by highlighting them the way you already highlight text. Balance answers the rest: whether the message reports a movement, a balance or both, which way the money went, which calendar the date is written in, whether a time sits beside it, and any line the rule must ignore. Nothing is installed until you ask, and you are shown the reading first, so a rule that picks the wrong number never reaches your history.
• The builder says what is still missing instead of failing later: a date highlighted without its time, a value with nothing around it to tell where the number ends, a bank or sender name longer than a rule can hold. A rule that another one already reads is named before you install it, so a second rule for a bank is a choice rather than a surprise.
• Rules you write can be sent for review: a preview screen shows the message, the sender and the whole rule before anything leaves the app, and nothing is sent unless you press send. A rule can also be exported as a file and shared however you like.
• Rule packs: import a rule another reader wrote from the "Your own packs" screen, and export the ones on your device. Every pack states where it came from and how it was checked.
• "Banks Balance reads": a screen now lists every bank Balance can read, which ones it is currently reading for you, and where each rule came from — the banks shipped with the app, plus rules other readers contributed, such as the one for an Italian bank that now ships with it. You can turn a bank off, and your choice is kept.
• A message from a sender Balance does not know can now be reported from anywhere in the app, so a bank you use can be added without hunting for the message that proves it.
• Balances and movements are now read from the words a bank actually wrote, rather than from the shape of one example message. Each bank's calendar is honoured, an impossible date is reported rather than quietly moved to a day the bank never mentioned, and a message the rules cannot read gives up quickly instead of holding up a balance scan.
• Your own rule and the packs on your device stay on the phone. Balance still declares no internet permission and makes no network requests: a pasted message used to write a rule is kept encrypted under a key that never leaves the device, is never copied into a backup, and is deleted when you install the rule or discard it. A pack moves only as a file you choose to send.
