New transaction history screen (experimental) summarizes every money movement across your banks.

• Marked as "History (Experimental)" in the footer — the feature is under active development.
• See the net sum plus separate deposits and withdrawals totals for today, this Persian month, and this year.
• The breakdown is grouped by Persian year and month; tap a year or a month to expand or collapse it, down to individual days.
• A movement is only recorded when the bank message states the resulting balance, and OTP-style payment prompts are ignored — only settled transactions end up in history.
• Duplicate deliveries of the same SMS (even with slightly different timestamps or reference numbers) are counted once, while separate movements of the same value stay distinct.
• History is scanned on its own, independently of the main balance view: it refreshes automatically and never blocks the balances, and older messages are backfilled too.
• Footer buttons are reordered so the most-used actions come first.
• Bank icons now keep their correct colors in dark theme.
