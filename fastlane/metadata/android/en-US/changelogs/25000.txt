CSV export of the transaction history, a pull-to-refresh that works from anywhere at the top, and a configurable freshness warning that flags balances not updated in a while.

• The History screen gained an Export button that saves your transactions as a CSV file through the system file picker. Each row carries the bank name, the account number, an ISO-8601 UTC timestamp, the date in the active calendar, the time of day, the raw signed rial amount, the amount as displayed and the currency unit, so the file stays machine-readable while still being easy to read in a spreadsheet.
• Pull-to-refresh on the main screen now also works when the pull starts anywhere on the bank list at the very top, not only on the total card.
• A first-run introduction now greets new installs: three short pages explain what Balance reads and how it stays offline and private, then ask for SMS access only there, in context — skippable at any time, gone for good afterwards, and re-openable from the About screen anytime.
• Balances that go unrefreshed now stand out: a bank whose last balance SMS is older than a configurable number of days gets an amber ring, an amber amount and an "N days" badge on its card, and an amber amount in the home-screen widget — so you always know how fresh today's totals really are before trusting them. The Display menu's new "Stale balance warning" selector sets the threshold: off, or 3, 7, 14 or 30 days. Balances without a dated SMS are never flagged.
• The first-run introduction now flows and navigates fully right-to-left in Persian (page swipes mirror the reading direction); the About screen hides its scrollbar, and the pull-to-refresh arrow snaps back more promptly.
