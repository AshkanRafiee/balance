Balance reads your bank's balance SMS on the phone and shows the latest balance for every supported bank — split per account number when the bank states one — plus the combined total. No account, no cloud, no analytics and no internet permission: the parsing happens on your device, and your financial data stays there, encrypted.

Currently built for users in Iran, with Iranian banks and their Persian message formats.

Features:
- Four bottom tabs: Balance, Payments, Savings (reserved for a future feature), and Settings. Settings contains About, Display, Data and Report
- A contextual first-run introduction explaining what is read and why; it is skippable, re-openable from Settings > About, and asks for SMS access there. All parsing is offline, with no network request
- Latest balance per bank, split per account when stated, without counting the same message twice, plus a combined total with optional account exclusions
- A manual Payments tab for one-off payments, subscriptions, debts and fixed loan installments. Choose weekly, monthly or yearly repeats and end by date or count; mark each occurrence paid or unpaid. It estimates included balances minus this month's unpaid plans and lists overdue plans separately
- Stop a plan after today while preserving its existing occurrences and paid states. Planned payments never make an actual payment, change bank balances or history, or match themselves against SMS transactions
- Toman (default), Rial as stored, or a custom currency name, with balances masked by default and hidden again on every open
- Automatic SMS updates, pull-to-refresh on the bank list or history, and a freshness warning for old balances
- Turning SMS access off deletes nothing — everything already read stays on screen, and a strip under the total card offers to grant access again
- Home-screen widget with the same bank order, totals and masking, in its own color theme, locking together with the app
- Transaction history by day, month and year, keeping out-of-order messages (such as a transfer and its fee) in their true order
- Amber "Unaccounted" rows when bank statements change beyond the movements explained by SMS; the app reports the difference without inventing missing transactions
- Filter the history by deposits or withdrawals and by date — today, this month, this year, or a custom range in the Persian or Gregorian calendar — and search it by any word
- Bank-stated movement reasons and channels — such as phone top-ups, bill payments, refunds and instant transfers — shown in your language and kept separate from your notes
- Private notes: tap any row to write your own words about that movement; they follow it through every filter, into CSV exports and into encrypted backups
- CSV export of the history shown at that moment, including your notes and the bank's stated reason and channel
- Password-encrypted backup and restore of balances, history, notes, planned payments, their payment states, reasons and channels
- A full SMS reset in Settings > Data, confirmed twice; it rebuilds SMS-derived balances and history while preserving manual payment plans and their payment states
- Optional in-app lock with PIN, password or fingerprint, covering the app, the widget and screenshots/recents
- English and Persian interface, Persian (Jalali) or Gregorian calendar, and a light or dark theme that follows the device
- Settings > Report shows which bank messages parse and which do not, and lets you send or copy exactly the ones you pick

Free and open source. If your bank's messages are not recognized, or a balance or account looks wrong, telling us — through Settings > Report, the issue tracker or email — is the fastest way to get it added.
