Balance reads bank SMS on your phone and shows the latest balance for supported banks — split per account when stated — plus the total. No account, cloud, analytics or internet permission: parsing is on-device and stored data is encrypted. Balance does not upload or send it; Report, sharing, CSV export and backup only hand selected data to the app or location you choose.

Currently built for users in Iran, with Iranian banks and their Persian message formats.

Features:
- A first-run introduction explaining what is read and why; skippable, and re-openable from About
- Offline SMS parsing
- Latest balance per bank, split per account when stated, without counting the same message twice
- A combined total, with accounts you can leave out
- Toman (default), Rial as stored, or a custom currency
- Balances masked by default for privacy, hidden again on every open
- Automatic balance updates as bank SMS arrive; history refreshes when you open its page and while it is open. Pull-to-refresh on the bank list refreshes balances; on history it refreshes balances and history
- A freshness warning: a bank whose last message is older than the window you set is flagged on its card and in the widget
- Turning SMS access off deletes nothing — everything already read stays on screen, and a strip under the total card offers to grant access again
- On Android 17, the system may hide restricted SMS from Balance; diagnostics makes clear when its results cover accessible messages only
- Home-screen widget with the same bank order and totals, its own privacy mask, light/dark mode, and selected theme color
- Transaction history by day, month and year, showing out-of-order messages (a transfer and its fee) in their true order
- Unaccounted money: when a balance statement changed by more than the messages explain, the difference appears as an amber "Unaccounted" row on the later statement's date and is counted in every total. It is proven by two statements your bank sent
- Filter the history by deposits or withdrawals, by date, and by exact reusable tags — today, this month, this year, or a custom range in the Persian or Gregorian calendar. Search across the transaction details and tags
- Bank-stated movement reasons and channels appear in your language when present, and stay separate from your note
- Payment merchants read from authorization SMS: when a purchase message names a merchant and uniquely matches a completed withdrawal, the history shows it as sourced payment information. An OTP alone never creates or confirms a transaction, and ambiguous matches stay unattached
- Private notes on any history row, including amber Unaccounted rows, plus multiple tags on movements: add your own words and labels, with notes following their row into filters, CSV exports and encrypted backups. A note on an Unaccounted row disappears with that derived row if a delayed SMS closes the gap
- Optional commitments card for recurring payments or receipts, with separate totals and reminders
- CSV export of exactly what the history shows at that moment, through the system file picker, with notes on movements or Unaccounted rows, movement tags, and the bank's reason, channel and matched merchant
- Password-encrypted backup and restore of balances, history, notes, tags, commitments, bank facts and recognized SMS source samples
- Optional in-app lock with PIN, password or fingerprint, covering the app, the widget and screenshots/recents
- English and Persian interface, Persian (Jalali) or Gregorian calendar, light or dark mode, and eight curated theme colors
- Report (Scan diagnostics): see which bank messages parse, choose what is wrong, and send or copy your samples; OTP values are removed

Free and open source. If a bank message or balance looks wrong, report it through the issue tracker or Report screen.
