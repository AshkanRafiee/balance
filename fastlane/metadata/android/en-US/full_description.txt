Balance reads your bank's balance SMS on the phone and shows the latest balance for every supported bank — split per account number when the bank states one — plus the combined total. No account, no cloud, no analytics and no internet permission: parsing happens on your device and your data stays encrypted there.

Iranian banks ship with the app, plus rules other readers wrote for banks in Italy and Jordan; others are added from user reports.

Features:
- If your bank isn't recognized, paste one of its messages, select the amount, balance and date inside it, and Balance writes the rule, tries it on that same message and shows what it read. It stays on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to send it for review
- A combined total, with any account you like left out of it
- The currency shown next to every amount: Toman (default), Rial as stored, or a custom name
- Balances masked by default for privacy, hidden again on every open
- Automatic updates as bank SMS arrive, plus pull-to-refresh on the bank list or the history screen (all of it, one bank, or one account)
- A freshness warning: a bank whose last message is older than the window you set (off / 3 / 7 / 14 / 30 days) is flagged on its card and in the widget
- Turning SMS access off deletes nothing — everything already read stays on screen, and a strip under the total card offers to grant access again
- Home-screen widget with the same bank order, totals and masking, in its own color theme, locking together with the app
- Transaction history by day, month and year, showing out-of-order messages (a transfer and its fee) in their true order
- Unaccounted money: when a balance statement changed by more than the messages explain, the difference appears as an amber "Unaccounted" row on the later statement's date and counts in every total. It is arithmetic on two statements your bank sent itself, so the app never invents a movement it cannot prove
- Filter the history by deposits or withdrawals and by date — today, this month, this year, or a custom range in the Persian or Gregorian calendar
- Movement reasons read from the bank: when a message names the event behind a movement — a top-up, a bill payment, a refund, an instant transfer — the row says so in your own language, as does the channel when the bank states that. Where it states nothing, nothing is added, and it never mixes into your note
- Private notes: tap any row to write your own words about that movement; they follow it through every filter, into CSV exports and into encrypted backups
- CSV export of exactly what the history shows at that moment, with UTC timestamps, the date in your calendar, each amount with its own currency code, your notes, the bank's reason and channel, and a column marking a movement or unaccounted money
- Password-encrypted backup and restore of balances, history, notes, reasons and channels
- Optional in-app lock with PIN, password or fingerprint, covering the app, the widget and screenshots/recents
- English and Persian interface, Persian (Jalali) or Gregorian calendar, and a light or dark theme that follows the device
- Report (Scan diagnostics): see which bank messages parse and which do not, and send or copy exactly the ones you pick — from any sender, not only the ones it misses
- Choose which banks Balance reads: turn one off and its messages are left alone, keeping every balance and history entry already recorded, and picking them back up when you switch it on
- A full reset in the data menu, confirmed twice, with the option to delete your notes as well

Free and open source. If a balance, account or history entry looks wrong, telling us — through the issue tracker, the Report screen or email — is the fastest way to get it right.
