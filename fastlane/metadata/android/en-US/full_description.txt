Balance reads supported bank balance SMS messages locally and shows the latest balance for each bank plus the combined total.

Currently designed for users in Iran, with support for Iranian banks and Persian bank SMS formats.

Features:
- Offline SMS parsing with no internet permission
- Latest balance per supported bank without duplicate accumulation
- Combined balance total
- Toman display mode
- Persistent privacy masking for balances
- Balances update automatically as bank SMS arrive, plus pull-to-refresh
- Home-screen widget with quick refresh and privacy mask
- Transaction history with deposit and withdrawal tracking per day, month and year
- Per-bank history: tap a bank to see just that bank's transactions
- Bank sorting by balance or update time, applied to the list and the widget
- Exclude banks from the combined total
- Automatic light and dark theme that follows your device
- Reset and rescan balances from the data menu
- Encrypted storage on device
- Password-protected backup and restore

SMS and financial data stay on the device, encrypted. No account, cloud service, analytics, or network access is required.
